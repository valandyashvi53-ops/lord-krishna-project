🌸 LORD KRISHNA

About Lord Krishna

Lord Krishna is one of the most loved and worshipped deities in Hinduism. He is the eighth incarnation (Avatar) of Lord Vishnu. He was born in Mathura and spent his childhood in Gokul and Vrindavan.

Krishna is known for his wisdom, kindness, bravery, love, and Beautiful flute music.He always protected truth and righteousness.

Birth Details

  • Father: Vasudeva
  • Mother: Devaki
  • Foster Parents: Nanda and Yashoda
  • Birth Place: Mathura
  • King Defeated: Kansa

Favourite Things

  1. Butter (Makhan)
  2. Milk
  3. Curd
  4. Flute
  5. Peacock Feather

Teachings

Lord Krishna taught the world that we should always perform our duty honestly without expecting rewards.

"You have the right to perform your duty, but never to the fruits of your actions."

🪷 Symbols of Lord Krishna

  • 🦚 Peacock Feather
  • 🪈 Flute
  • 🐄 Cow
  • 🪷 Lotus

🎉 Festivals

  • 🙏 Janmashtami
  • 🎨 Holi

  • 📖 Gita Jayanti

Bhagavad Gita Message

The Bhagavad Gita teaches us to live a life of honesty, discipline, devotion, and selfless service. Lord Krishna guided Arjuna during the Mahabharata and inspired millions of people with his divine knowledge.
